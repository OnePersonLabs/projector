# What Projector 3 takes from the pile

**Observed outcome:** the user stopped evaluation for quota. The OpenSpec arm
completed all three checkpoints and independent review. The Projector arm was
stopped in checkpoint 1 after its sandbox context/acceptance failure. The table
below records decisions and intended tests, not a claim that every contender
was exercised in both arms. Explicit specialist knowledge and richer retrieval
have focused code tests but no completed comparative benefit result. The
deterministic format conversion did preserve all 202 records. Project-aware
candidate review was not completed. See [results](rehearsal/results.md).

The 40-page pile synthesis is a design source, not proof of the implementation. This disposition belongs to the September 20 delivery campaign. Accepted product meaning lives in `.projector`; source evidence remains in intake. The useful goal is coherent accepted change per total cost, including setup, repair, attention and maintenance.

Its MIDI/audio/provenance implementation must not become Projector's architecture. Retain generic authenticated evidence handling where used. |

The materialization proposal explicitly says that two compilation responsibilities do not require two large compiler frameworks. The persistent-model proposal explicitly calls for a strong explicit-knowledge baseline and says weights must not own exact IDs, current membership or authority. These qualifications matter as much as the ambitions.

The initial paired run carries each implementation through three checkpoints. Its result may recommend OpenSpec. A prettier artifact format alone does not justify choosing Projector; all critical obligations must hold and the added consequence handling, continuation or review benefit must pay for its total cost.

Observed before redesign: a model-only update emitted full envelope/history payloads, repeatedly observed the repository, and lost freshness when an unrelated tracked rehearsal file changed between capture and planning. Representation inspection also differed between the installed runner and direct services. These are costs and defects to resolve, not benefits attributed to governance.
