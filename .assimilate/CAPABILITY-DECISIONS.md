The materialization proposal explicitly says that two compilation responsibilities do not require two large compiler frameworks. The persistent-model proposal explicitly calls for a strong explicit-knowledge baseline and says weights must not own exact IDs, current membership or authority. These qualifications matter as much as the ambitions.

The initial paired run carries each implementation through three checkpoints. Its result may recommend OpenSpec. A prettier artifact format alone does not justify choosing Projector; all critical obligations must hold and the added consequence handling, continuation or review benefit must pay for its total cost.

Observed before redesign: a model-only update emitted full envelope/history payloads, repeatedly observed the repository, and lost freshness when an unrelated tracked rehearsal file changed between capture and planning. Representation inspection also differed between the installed runner and direct services. These are costs and defects to resolve, not benefits attributed to governance.

## Fidelity and scope record

The **separate pile pack** starts from 38 Markdown inputs (`audit/PROCESSING.md`, 177,664 whitespace-delimited words) and produces a 40-Markdown-page synthesis. Its source map is `intake/pile-synthesis/audit/SOURCES.md`. S07 is malformed, S11 is empty, S16 is incomplete, and 15 referenced generated files are absent. The current capability decisions preserve these limits.

The **captured intake corpus** is different: 12 chats, 56 captured turn units, and 15 captured attachment hashes. That fidelity review is complete. This pass did not repeat it. The six current assimilation topics cover the main paths in the pile. Persistent assimilation maps to `topics/assimilation-workspace.md`; context compilation maps to `topics/purpose-built-context.md`; persistent learned judgment and curriculum map to `topics/persistent-project-understanding.md`. Conceptual dynamics maps to `topics/semantic-dynamics.md`; semantic control and materialization map to `topics/project-stewardship-and-change.md`; work economics and quality-frontier execution map to `topics/quality-and-work-economics.md`. The quality topic also keeps reusable selectors, tests, skills, and transformations as candidate low-burden artifacts. These files are a self-contained working synthesis, not accepted Projector specifications.

The pack and current topics retain distinct evidence, authority, and behavior claims. They distinguish conception from code or representation, evidence from interpretation, and observation from authority. They preserve the difference between surface variation, meaningful transformation, and authorized revision. They also distinguish relevance from impact, exact targeting from complete coverage, exposure from understanding, explicit records from learned weights, and deterministic execution from model judgment. Work routes include total cost, not only first-turn expense.

The high-level Phlatland lens-based explorer has no native equivalent in the six topics or CLI. It remains a future capability. Text-based evidence navigation is the narrower current support. Session-relative routing remains a proposal, not an accepted Projector obligation.

Keep general governance exceptions, purpose-specific exclusions, and authorized purpose changes distinct. `GovernanceException` is a scoped exception record; it does not define a reusable purpose-specific exclusion policy or authorize a purpose change. No such exclusion policy is implemented. Reconsider one only when a concrete, recurring authorized task shows that its accepted purpose requires a conditional exclusion that current scope and governance records cannot express. Define the purpose, excluded subject class, selector, authority, activating condition, review/expiry, invalidation, re-entry, and evidence before adopting it. A purpose change still uses its separate authorized change path. Model confidence or a stale approval does not authorize either one.

Projector does not claim to replace OpenSpec or manage every task. Arbitrary skills, models, tools, and transforms are not freely composable. The system does not simulate all semantics, run a project-trained model, provide a live spatial explorer, or execute software through a model-native runtime. These capabilities remain outside the current implementation. The table gives the trigger for reconsidering each deferred capability.

The historical 202-record conversion result belongs only to deterministic format conversion in the September 20 rehearsal. It does not prove current archive fidelity or implementation quality. It is not a reason to resume the halted comparison. The completed 12-chat review does not cover the separate 38-input pack. Review that pack again only if a concrete new gap is found.
