# Working synthesis

> **Current ownership:** This Markdown fileset is assimilation's own working synthesis. Its topic notes and references do not share identity, schema, lifecycle, or authority with Projector's accepted concept records. Some topics describe ideas *about* Projector, including its concept system. That subject matter does not make them Projector records.

This is the self-contained working synthesis maintained by assimilation. It explores a development system that can retain, refine, and apply a complex conception without requiring any one agent to hold it all at once. It preserves an immediately useful assimilation workflow and a more ambitious Projector research direction. It is not an accepted Projector implementation specification or a claim that the proposed mechanisms already work.

## Start here

The desired experience is simple: supply a pile of material, discuss what matters, and return later to a coherent understanding that has developed rather than evaporated. The system takes responsibility for organization, meaningful connections, and continuity. The user should not have to maintain the machinery that makes that possible.

## Governing distinctions

Cost-conscious work does **not** mean automatic quality downgrades when quota is low. Required quality stays fixed; choose an effective route based on total expected cost, including mistakes, reconstruction, review, and integration. Scarcity-driven compromises require an explicit user choice.

A portable synthesis states what the design means, not who said what. Shared ancestry and revisions matter to accurate reconciliation, but ordinary readers do not need the conversation history. No original attachment or source chat is required to understand this file set.

An interesting mechanism is not automatically a requirement. Markdown, learned models, graphs, swarms, and compilers must be assessed against the capabilities they actually contribute. Preserve ambitious goals without representing an untested mechanism as established fact.
