## ORBIT/C2: The targeting solution is an executable change program

**The metaphor carries a technical distinction.** The repository is the planet. Orbital ion-cannon satellites identify concrete regions to retire, replace, create, refactor, preserve, and validate, then realize a coordinated transformation. They do not individually land, reconstruct the mission, and improvise unrelated edits. The ambition is predesigned, precise change at project scale, not merely faster local editing.

The assistant called the candidate mechanism an **executable change program**; the assistant-authored research request also floated **Change IR**, an intermediate representation, without settling a schema. Its role is to connect intent and relevant meaning to operations with machine-resolvable targets, dependencies, preconditions, preserved behavior, and verification obligations.

The distinguishing contrast is:

```text
Task: replace the old implementation and update consumers.

Change instruction:
  migrate resolved callers of InstrumentFactory.create;
  bind reference-query membership and contracts to observed state;
  edit identified call nodes and necessary imports;
  preserve accepted InstrumentPort behavior;
  reject ambiguous targets or invalidated assumptions;
  check compatibility and absence of obsolete callers.
```

This is an illustrative contract, not an implemented format. Targets may be declarations, symbols, configuration entries, registrations, generated outputs, or relationships. Files and folders are insufficient: one capability spans artifacts, while one file may embody multiple responsibilities.

**Why it matters.** Existing packet containers can faithfully execute an inadequate decomposition. R3 exposes a boundary between packaging proposals and deriving them. The assistant recommended strengthening this seam rather than adding another large planning apparatus. That is a diagnosis supported by the inspected module's structure, not proof that no targeting logic exists elsewhere.

A prose specification, dependency DAG, or type-aware codemod alone is not equivalent to this proposed capability. The thread's OpenRewrite comparison supplied a transformation ingredient, not evidence that it solves intent or impact completeness. The representation and compiler organization remain replaceable; the surviving need is reliable translation from agreed meaning into bounded effects.

## ORBIT/C3: Precision, coverage, and intention are separate achievements

The assistant separated three questions that “almost deterministic” can accidentally collapse:

| Question | Proposed treatment |
|---|---|
| Can the selected target be hit exactly? | Resolve supported representations, bind inputs, apply a supported operation, and reject mismatches. |
| Are these all the relevant targets? | Use semantic bindings, analyzers, observations, and declared boundaries; retain unknown dependencies rather than asserting closure. |
| Does the change realize the right intention? | Use stewardship, meaningful acceptance conditions, necessary experiments, and evidence capable of challenging the implementation. |

A reference resolver can identify a call without establishing every behavioral expectation surrounding it. Exact targeting therefore does not prove complete impact discovery. Deterministic execution of an incorrect plan remains an incorrect change.

The proposed aim is **bounded uncertainty with precise execution**, not universal semantic decidability. Exploratory work is not categorically rejected; uncontrolled exploration across supposedly settled parts of the project is what the design seeks to avoid. Discoveries may change the proposal, while unaffected decisions and work remain reusable.

R4's executable-lens requirement supports another important distinction: handwritten code may vary while satisfying executable predicates. Exact output is required only where a lens owns deterministic generation. The proposal need not imply a perfect specification from which the entire repository is regenerated.

These qualifications constrain the assistant's interpretation, not the user's ambition to improve precision dramatically. How much closure can actually be established, especially around dynamic or unmodeled relationships, remains unresolved. No completeness or scalability result was produced.

## ORBIT/C4: State-bound targeting includes queries and shared assumptions

**The decisive example:** a planner finds three consumers of a deprecated interface; a fourth appears while the replacement is being prepared. The original three files have unchanged hashes, yet the removal plan is stale.

The plan therefore needs dependencies on **query results and membership**, including meaningful empty results, not only on previously discovered files. R4's scoped-invalidation requirement already describes this principle, including new consumers and reuse across unrelated changes. The assistant's Bazel comparison supported dependency-accounted reuse as an analogy; it did not verify Projector's implementation.

The same reasoning constrains parallelism. Disjoint writes are insufficient when one worker changes a contract another worker read. Scheduling must respect shared assumptions and read dependencies as well as write collisions. R4 describes parallel preparation or isolated worktrees with coordinated mutation of shared governed state. It explicitly concerns cooperating writers, not protection against arbitrary same-user interference.

**A concrete unresolved mismatch:** R3 rejects overlap even when dependencies order packets. A sequence that removes, replaces, and refactors the same region needs either a compound packet owning that region throughout, or richer ordered transformations with intermediate-state checks. Both were proposed; neither was selected or implemented. More workers do not remove this representational constraint.

The expected benefit is selective refresh rather than global invalidation, while refusing to reuse work whose assumptions no longer hold. It depends on adequate dependency capture; hashes and leases are not evidence of complete knowledge or absence of interference.

## ORBIT/C5: One coordinated evolution, not destruction first

The user imagined obsolete structures being removed before replacements were laid down. The assistant proposed retaining the coordinated outcome while changing the mutation strategy: prepare and validate replacements in a **shadow repository state**, then promote the coherent result.

The illustrative progression was contract/impact establishment, replacement preparation and mechanical rewrites, composition in a shadow tree, validation, obsolete-path retirement and absence checks, then promotion. Ordering may vary by change. The important distinction is between the user's coherent experience and the machinery's controlled intermediate states.

“One progressive iterative pass” was interpreted as one coordinated evolution with local iteration when evidence demands it, not a guarantee that every operation executes once without discovery. A failed adapter check should not cause global rediscovery of the mission.

This interpretation was an assistant correction, not an explicit user retreat from the original vision. Shadow trees are a candidate mechanism, not established Projector behavior here. Repository promotion also does not make external-service or database effects atomic; those need their own migration and recovery arrangements.

The proposal seeks to avoid exposing destructive intermediate states and to localize rework. Whether its staging overhead is justified for a particular change remains unevaluated. No general rollback, semantic equivalence, or atomicity guarantee was demonstrated.

## ORBIT/C6: Satellites are execution capabilities; savings come from retained understanding

The assistant's strongest economic reformulation was to associate a satellite with an **execution capability**, not necessarily an LLM. Mechanical transformations, generators, analyzers, compilers, validators, and integration tools can perform much of the work. Models remain appropriate for unfamiliar-subsystem investigation, design decisions, and genuinely new behavior within established contracts.

The causal proposal is: settle reusable understanding, synthesize novel parts, mechanically transform supported parts, and retain selectors/contracts/validators/transforms so later changes do not pay for the same reasoning again. Useful parallelism is supplementary. Eliminating repeated cognition is the deeper expected gain; accumulating more prose is not equivalent.

## Synthesis closure

### Core thesis from this thread

The proposed advance is a project steward that turns sufficiently shared intent into a precise executable change, rather than merely assigning better-described tasks. Correct targeting, not agent count, is the assistant's candidate unifying insight.

### Load-bearing insights and distinctions to preserve

The user explicitly wants ownership of understanding, precise coordinated replacement/refactoring, and efficiency. Preserve that ambition separately from the assistant's replaceable mechanisms. C2's proposal packaging/derivation distinction, C3's precision/coverage/intention split, C4's query membership example, and C5's coordinated evolution versus literal single-pass execution are essential qualifications.

### Candidate mechanisms, not sacred architecture

Change IR, shadow trees, compound packets, ordered transforms, particular interview skills, and fleet topology remain candidates. Their purposes are recoverable understanding, bounded effects, valid reuse, coherent integration, and justified effort. Persistent holons and a new workflow store are not established prerequisites.

### Questions the final synthesis must resolve

Architectural questions: how is the targeting solution actually derived; what coverage can be established; when is discovery necessary; how does stewardship relate to existing canonical state; and does modeling overhead earn its cost? Reconcile the workflow-store conflict and staged ownership issue. Exact serialization formats and worker counts can remain implementation choices.

### Relationship to current Projector

The assistant favored connecting and strengthening the existing meaning, planning, and execution machinery rather than adding another planning layer. That recommendation depends on R1–R5. Later assessment should verify the proposal provider, public composition/orchestration, and applicable authority model; constructor code and retained requirements alone do not establish the complete experience.

### Suggested synthesis posture

Treat this as intent, observations, hypotheses, and reasoning, not a binding specification. Merge, replace, challenge, or reject mechanisms while addressing the needs and distinctions they carry. Do not manufacture user approval, count inherited claims as corroboration, or mistake a coherent proposal for demonstrated correctness or economic superiority.
