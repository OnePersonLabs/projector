## 3. Substantive contributions

### QFE/C1. Quality frontier, not scarcity adaptation

**Explicit user intent:** automate selection without manual “stick shift” supervision. Seek the highest useful competence whose next upgrade does not justify its incremental cost, while avoiding lower settings reasonably expected to produce worse work or greater eventual correction costs. This is neither maximum effort everywhere nor the cheapest route that sometimes passes tests. [T; P §§3–4]

The user reports that conserving allowance through weaker execution has previously consumed the remaining quota, seeded low-quality changes, and increased repair work after reset. This is experiential evidence, not a measured model comparison. The user explicitly rejects normal quota-awareness and allows, at most, an optional manual emergency lever.

P proposes a normal-policy invariant: identical task, evidence, available capabilities, and preferences imply the same route regardless of remaining allowance or time until reset. Near exhaustion should not lower standards; approaching reset should not trigger gratuitous extra work. Actual capability loss is different: use an already-qualified alternative or preserve resumable state and pause.

“Finish less, not worse” is the assistant's proposed interpretation of the manual lever: restrict new admission or defer optional work, preserving accepted scope and evidence requirements. Deferral must remain visible. This implementation was not separately approved by the user.

Quality remains multidimensional: correctness, completeness, architecture, maintainability, failure handling, evidence, and unresolved liabilities. One material deficit cannot be averaged away by easy test passes. How to establish adequate quality without expensive judgment remains unresolved; P supplies a policy, not a calibrated frontier.

### QFE/C2. Allocate whole execution plans and charge the whole outcome

Choosing model, effort, partition, context, verification, and topology separately can create false savings. A cheaper worker may require expensive context preparation or a complete owner rewrite; parallel speed can disappear in integration. P therefore proposes comparing **whole candidate execution plans**, including leaving work with the current owner. [P §§1, 3, 14–15]

Lifecycle cost includes owner and worker work, routing, duplicated context, consultation, verification, worktree setup, integration, observed rework, and unresolved downstream exposure. Integrated completion time and human correction burden also matter. Main-pool usage, separate allowances, API spend, and local resources should remain distinguishable rather than falsely converted into one interchangeable currency.

Charge a cheap worker's review and owner rewrite to that route. Link later attributable regressions to earlier outcomes, marking uncertain attribution. Avoid double-counting inclusive parent usage and child totals, or observed repairs and already-realized liability estimates. Unknown future cost is not zero.

Reject inadequate candidates first; compare nondominated quality/cost/time alternatives afterward. Additional material quality can justify an upgrade even when a weaker route usually finishes. Uncertain quality equivalence favors the stronger established route; uncertain coordination benefits favor the simpler topology. These are proposed conservative rules, not empirical optima.

The earlier assistant's fixed “2.5× activity” break-even shortcut is explicitly rejected in P: token mix, caching, and total work differ between plans. Earlier percentage allocations and numerical escalation weights were also uncalibrated illustrations, not measurements to inherit.

### QFE/C3. Preserve a strong owner over a coherence region

The recommendation evolved materially. The assistant initially favored Sol High with stronger consultants, assuming most work would be bounded execution. The user challenged that assumption for current Projector work and cited the wrap-up plan. The assistant ultimately recommended **GPT-6 Astra / High** as persistent implementation owner, a judgment based on the described convergence workload, not inspection of that missing plan. [T; P §5]

The surviving rationale: architectural decisions and implementation are not always separable. Delegating each difficult question can lose context and leave a weaker owner responsible for noticing when its understanding is wrong. A strong owner should implement tightly coupled work directly, not become a ceremonial manager that rewrites delegates' output.

P calls the relevant unit a **coherence region**: work sharing active assumptions, contracts, and debugging state. Phase labels supply defaults; coherent capsule boundaries permit exceptions. Keep the route stable until its basis changes materially, rather than reroute every tool call or freeze one choice for the entire project.

XHigh remains a bounded candidate when additional reasoning has a specific unresolved question to address. It is not automatic insurance for every important task. De-escalation needs a stable boundary and evidence, not one passing test.

Persistent ownership does not mean an endless transcript. Refresh from current, state-bound evidence when accumulated context becomes stale or noisy. The savings from retaining context versus refreshing it remain workload-dependent. [P §10]

### QFE/C4. Make delegation genuinely bounded and independently checkable

The central inversion is **remove uncertainty before buying cheaper execution**, rather than transfer uncertainty to a weaker model and escalate after damage. P proposes a **delegation envelope** projected through the existing Execution Capsule, not a parallel authority document. [P §§6–7, 10–11]

It carries observable objectives, non-goals, relevant state/contract/fixture bindings, source references, negative constraints, unresolved questions, decision authority, read/write/effect boundaries, return requirements, verification, and discoveries requiring a stop. Workers may propose results but cannot silently settle architecture, broaden scope, suppress anomalies, or adjust acceptance to match their output.

Qualification belongs to **task envelope + verifier + backend**, not a model name or broad label such as “adapter work.” A familiar task with a new concurrency assumption falls outside prior qualification. Worker confidence does not establish competence.

Read-only outputs can still corrupt downstream decisions. An incomplete inventory may omit required impact; deterministic enumeration should anchor completeness where feasible. A scan of a mutating checkout is not a coherent snapshot merely because it records the starting commit. Use immutable inputs or track actual read dependencies and invalidate affected claims. Evidence locators remain available behind summaries.

Choose worker and verifier together. Generated tests cannot exclusively define correctness; accepted requirements, historical evidence, independent fixtures, and candidate expectations have different causal origins. Another instance of the same model is not automatically an independent oracle.

Escalate the **failure class**, not the retry count: retrieve missing evidence, repair an environment, resolve a contract dispute, improve an inadequate oracle, or change diagnosis when edits repeat without new evidence. Additional reasoning cannot substitute for inaccessible facts. If delegation packaging and checking exceed local completion cost, retain the already-informed owner.

### QFE/C5. Parallelism requires both semantic independence and economic benefit

The user questions worktree policies that consume more quota without worthwhile speed, sometimes slowing completion. P's proposed two gates are **semantic admissibility** and **lifecycle usefulness**. Independence makes a split eligible; it does not make splitting desirable. [P §§8–9]

Use semantic reads/writes, contract dependencies and volatility, verification closure, shared resources, context overlap, critical-path contribution, and integration readiness. Disjoint files can still encode incompatible interpretations of one format. Shared reads of an immutable accepted contract need not block concurrency. Unresolved shared meaning argues for keeping work together until a useful boundary exists.

Keep four decisions distinct: **delegation, concurrency, worktree creation, and model change**. A sequential specialist may be economical; an independent investigation may need no writing checkout; parallel deterministic tests may need no additional model; a warm owner may need no delegation.

Compare setup, constrained execution, contention, integration queues, reconciliation, validation, and repair against sequential integrated completion. Child completion is not the finish line. Require a material benefit beyond estimate uncertainty; resource-intensive speedups need a stable speed preference, not spare quota. P's timing examples are hypothetical, not Codex measurements.

The proposed scheduler limits work in progress by integration capacity rather than worker slots. One additional qualified writer is an initial experiment, not a permanent cap. Avoid rigid waves when completed work can safely integrate and unblock dependencies.

**Integration is part of the proposal, not an assumed Git property.** Freeze worker writes, check actual effects and relevant input validity, stage the combined revision, run affected checks, then publish through one authority. A clean merge does not preserve semantic validity; changed contracts invalidate dependent claims selectively.

Worktrees do not isolate shared services, ports, databases, or external effects. Shared manifests, contracts, lockfiles, and registries need explicit ownership. Required ignored plans and environment inputs must be materialized. Native children, exec workers, and descendants need common admission and cancellation controls; cancellation must acknowledge actual termination before releasing ownership. Instructions and post-run diffs cannot substitute for enforceable effect boundaries. These safeguards are proposed launch qualifications, not verified host guarantees.

### QFE/C7. Learn from integrated outcomes, not flattering completion statistics

P proposes simple rules and compact telemetry first, with learned routing only if it adds value. Record policy/version/state, task difficulty, actual backend binding, resources, topology, integration, findings, human correction, acceptance, and later linked defects. Qualification is scoped and revocable after material changes to models, prompts, tools, contracts, or task families. [P §12]

The decisive baseline is a **competent single Astra High owner with the same context and verification improvements**. Otherwise routing receives credit for better specification. Match tasks and difficulty: cheap models receiving easy tasks creates selection bias. Use held-out representative changes, adversarial negatives, follow-up windows, and reopened outcomes for later attributable defects. Do not test every possible model/effort/topology combination.

The claim under test is preserved or materially improved quality at justified lifecycle cost, not whether a child runs successfully. Unknown equivalence should not license experiments on valuable canonical state. Stronger routes also need to earn their additional cost.

P contains 28 proposed policy acceptance scenarios, including quota neutrality, wrong self-authored tests, stale snapshots, cross-contract conflicts, integration rewrites, silent fallback, and cancellation. R reports three assistant self-review/revision passes plus a final consistency check. These establish neither independent consensus nor passing runtime tests. Comparative savings and scalability remain unmeasured.

### QFE/C8. Retain reasoning so future work needs less of it

P's longer-horizon hypothesis is that recurring expensive discoveries can become scoped contracts, recognizers, impact selectors, fixtures, integration preconditions, or evidenced task-family routes. This would reduce recurring uncertainty rather than merely reroute it more cheaply. [P §13]

The idea inherits Projector's proposed crystallization of cognition into executable knowledge. Semantic holons could contribute ownership and invariant concerns, but neither holons nor a neural Persistent System Conception are prerequisites. They must not acquire standing agents or competing schedulers by implication.

Learning has a failure mode: a mistaken fix can become an efficient validator enforcing the mistake. Promotion therefore remains governed, independently evidenced, replayed against history and negative cases, and invalidatable. Measure repeated-change marginal cost before claiming amortization.

P proposes extending existing planning, capsule, execution, evidence, and integration surfaces, with lightweight policy/capability/outcome records where useful. It defers broad routing and learned optimization until a strong-owner baseline, one qualified leaf route, and one real parallel seam discriminate benefits. This is the source's proposed investment sequence, not a new implementation instruction. Remove routing machinery that costs more than it saves while retaining useful context and verification improvements. [P §§14, 18]

## 4. Integration map and unresolved tensions

**Work economics:** C1–C2 sharpen efficiency into stable quality/lifecycle tradeoffs rather than balance-sensitive rationing. The inherited Work Economics excerpt explicitly warns against mandating a dedicated subsystem. It also keeps `opl-borg` separate: a shared principle or distribution repository does not imply shared authority or runtime dependency. This thread proposes no Borg implementation change.

**Semantic planning and progressive decisions:** C3–C5 reuse state-bound context, scoped validity, and materiality. Routine routing need not create another canonical entity or global decision record.

**Persistent understanding and holons:** C3/C8 share concern for accumulated understanding but do not establish equivalence with trained project memory or concept controllers. Their feasibility and benefit remain separate questions.

**Throughput:** C5 revises occupancy-driven parallelism, not coherent decomposition or independence itself. **Evaluation:** C7 must distinguish router value from improved envelopes and verification. The strongest unresolved objection is that one competent owner with those same improvements may outperform the entire routing apparatus.

## 5. Synthesis closure

### Core thesis from this thread

Preserve justified competence and reduce avoidable uncertainty, duplication, and coordination. Judge allocation by integrated quality and lifecycle work, not cheaper turns or busy agents.

### Load-bearing insights and distinctions to preserve

Explicit user constraints are unattended operation, quality-sensitive total efficiency, no normal scarcity-driven downgrades, and economically justified parallelism. Strong ownership, model rankings, envelopes, and routing records remain hypotheses or candidate mechanisms. File isolation, semantic independence, independent evidence, and economic advantage are different properties.

### Candidate mechanisms, not sacred architecture

Coherence regions, capsule envelopes, phase defaults, capability probes, topology gates, telemetry, and learned routes are replaceable. Their purposes may be met inside existing machinery or by a simpler fixed policy. Do not create one subsystem per contribution.

### Questions the final synthesis must resolve

Can quality equivalence be established cheaply enough to delegate? Where does persistent ownership pay more than specialization? What can the actual host enforce and attest? Do routing and parallelism beat equally well-supported single ownership? These affect architecture; exact model names, weights, and concurrency limits can wait for evidence.

### Relationship to current Projector

The proposed delta is execution-policy selection within the described semantic planning loop, plus stronger lifecycle accounting and integration controls. Actual capsule, graph, verifier, and backend capabilities require later verification. The unseen wrap-up plan cannot substantiate current workload claims.

### Suggested synthesis posture

Treat this as intent, reasoning, and bounded design evidence, not an approved specification. Merge, simplify, substitute, or reject mechanisms while explicitly addressing the user corrections. Do not count shared excerpts or self-review as independent confirmation; do not let a polished proposal conceal missing comparative evidence.
