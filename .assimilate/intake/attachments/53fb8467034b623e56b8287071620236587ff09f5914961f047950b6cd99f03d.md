## 3. Substantive contributions

### CTXVIEW/C1: Reference semantic identity, not storage location

**Origin and need:** T1 asks for concept-per-file Markdown references and filesystem-compatible concept naming, initially favoring an uncommon emoji. T2 proposes a distinct textual namespace:

```text
§instrument-adapter-contract
    initially resolves to concepts/instrument-adapter-contract.md
```

The assistant's rationale is that a concept should remain referenceable independently of where its current representation lives. A resolver could later use an index or aliases instead of a fixed directory. This is proposed indirection, not an implemented identity service.

Suggested canonical filenames use lowercase ASCII kebab-case, matching `[a-z0-9]+(?:-[a-z0-9]+)*`, plus `.md`; a human display name can differ. The graph, rather than directory nesting, would carry semantic hierarchy. Rename and alias semantics were not specified.

The assistant favored `§` for visual distinction, low accidental collision, and simple parsing, contrasting semantic references with `@file`, `$skill`, and `/command`. These host examples were conversational context, not verified cross-host contracts. Claimed ergonomic, Unicode, and tokenization advantages were not benchmarked. `[[concept-name]]` remained a wiki-link alternative.

Bare `§id` was initially preferred; bracketed forms sparked the user's broader idea. T2 suggested `§[id|label]` and `§[id#section]`; T4 later illustrated:

```text
§[instrument-adapter-contract?view=review&focus=lifecycle]
```

This separates identity from requested view. T4 would leave recipient state, revisions, receipts, and budgets to the tool session rather than encode everything in prose. No grammar or parser was finalized, and Markdown itself would not execute these requests. **The namespace distinction matters more than the particular sigil.**

### CTXVIEW/C2: Render concepts into decision-relevant working perspectives

**User seed:** T3 imagines concepts held in a repo-side model collaborating with a frontier model, or temporarily represented in the frontier model's context. Prose can express different perspectives on the same concept. The recipient's interpretation depends on its existing conceptual associations and inferred gaps; identical text does not guarantee identical understanding.

The user's NeRF/view-frustum analogy preserves a crucial distinction: **the concept is not exhausted by one rendering**. Position, orientation, and field of view evoke selective access to a richer underlying organization. It is an explanatory metaphor, not a proposal to use graphics transforms or a demonstrated mathematical model of meaning.

T4 calls the candidate mechanism a **Semantic Context Compiler**: concepts and relationships, combined with task, perspective, scope, recipient, and current context, produce a working view and handles for expansion. “Conceptbase” and “oracle” were the user's provisional service names, not commitments to separate components.

The contrast is not merely retrieving relevant pages versus retrieving better pages. The service would assume explicit responsibility for selecting and expressing the interpretation needed for work, while retaining access to its basis. Whether this materially surpasses strong retrieval remains open.

An assistant refinement is **decision-conditioned rather than merely role-conditioned** rendering. “For a programmer” is broad; “for deciding whether behavior belongs in the adapter or test harness” is precise. The view must also retain governing constraints originating outside the apparent task. Otherwise a narrow perspective can erase precisely what makes an action invalid.

T4 suggests Markdown plus relationships as an initial backing, with a learned representation potentially behind the same interface later. This is a possible staging choice, not a user rejection of the richer repo-side model. The relationship between persistent learned conception and textual rendering remains a valuable unresolved seed.

### CTXVIEW/C3: Disclosure frontiers, not generic summaries

**User proposal:** Supply minimum context plus enough information to recognize when additional detail is needed. Inline material, references with read guidance, or tool queries could all support this.

T4 names a **disclosure frontier**: the boundary beyond which a supplied view is no longer sufficient, paired with conditions that make expansion necessary. Its illustrative packet can be compressed as:

```text
Concept: §instrument-adapter-contract
Purpose: Separate instrument behavior from platform implementation.
Constraint: Shared callers must not depend on platform-specific types.
Sufficient for: Choosing boundaries and locating affected callers.
Omitted: Lifecycle, shutdown ordering, failure recovery.
Expand before: Creating/destroying instances or changing those behaviors.
Request: §[instrument-adapter-contract?view=implementation&focus=lifecycle]
```

These are example semantics, not inspected requirements of an actual adapter.

A summary says what little is known; a frontier additionally says **when that little stops being enough**. Expected leverage comes from avoiding premature detail without requiring the recipient to discover every omission unaided.

The frontier should describe the relevant neighborhood, not reproduce the entire repository index. Neither compiler nor agent has complete awareness of unknowns. T4 therefore proposes broader-search escape routes and checks that do not depend solely on the agent requesting help. The mechanism is replaceable; its purpose is actionable omission, not merely short summaries. No frontier-generation reliability was demonstrated.

### CTXVIEW/C4: Compile specialized delegations without recursive summary degradation

T3 imagines orchestrators equipped with guidance about concepts and context construction, creating purpose-built children with their own rendered concept sets and disclosure guidance. The desired capability is deliberately constructed working perspectives, not indiscriminate instruction inheritance.

T4 shifts repetitive prompt construction from the parent to reusable machinery. A parent declares the child's objective, delegated and forbidden decisions, scope, and completion evidence. The compiler supplies a small operating protocol, applicable constraints, task contract, joint concept views, tools, and disclosure frontier. This is an assistant proposal, not an approved delegation interface.

Two refinements carry the causal argument. First, children should render from the canonical basis, not only the parent's summary: repeated summarization can compound omissions. Second, render concept sets **jointly**: excellent descriptions of A and B can omit the decisive relationship, such as A's lifetime governing B's validity.

This makes source access and relationships important without specifying that the source must be a particular database or neural model. The assistant also challenges “lots of agents” as an objective: useful isolation and parallelism must justify coordination and context-construction costs. No worker count, orchestration topology, or superiority over fewer workers was established.

### CTXVIEW/C5: Context receipts and semantic supplementation are not knowledge guarantees

**User proposal:** Track what each subagent has received so subsequent queries return complementary information rather than another largely redundant explanation.

T4 distinguishes a **working representation**, the model's temporary interpretation, from a **context ledger**, externally tracked delivery and presence. Candidate states include externally available, currently present, previously supplied but no longer present, superseded, and behaviorally exercised in a relevant check. None implies general comprehension.

The tracking unit should be the **context instance**, not only a durable agent ID. Compaction, reset, or source revision can invalidate the assumption that an earlier packet is still usable. The authority and update mechanics of this ledger were not specified.

A lifecycle request could return newly relevant rules, their relationship to supplied ownership constraints, and exceptions, without repeating the adapter introduction. But **semantic supplementation is not textual subtraction**. A short repeated anchor may be necessary to attach a new rule correctly. Eliminating all repeated words can undermine comprehension.

The user's “idempotent precision” aspiration is therefore split into two claims. Deduplicating an identified patch when assembling host context is a software property one could implement. Repeated exposure yielding identical interpretation or reliable application is a behavioral hypothesis. No introspective “grokked it” meter was proposed or demonstrated. Ledger maintenance and reset recovery remain costs, not free savings.

### CTXVIEW/C6: Learn when to preload, disclose, or repair an explanation

T3's hypothetical pattern is an initial query followed by X for some agents and Y for others, with cached supplements and feedback improving future delivery. The illustrative half-X/half-Y split is not observed telemetry.

T4's causal refinement is that appending both X and Y to every initial answer recreates broad context. The useful question is what predicts the branch:

| Pattern | Candidate response |
|---|---|
| Nearly every successful run immediately requires X | Preload X |
| Different task categories predict X or Y | Condition the initial view |
| The branch depends on a discovery during execution | Keep targeted disclosure triggers |
| Follow-ups arise from ambiguous wording | Repair the initial explanation |

Caching and adaptive preload selection are related but distinct. Follow-up frequency alone is not an adequacy signal: a query may indicate successful disclosure, while no query may conceal a missing premise. The assistant proposes learning from **queries plus outcomes**, including silent failures.

The suggested objective is minimum expected end-to-end cost subject to task quality and preserved constraints. Count rendering, inference, retrieval latency, repeated work, and correction, not merely initial tokens. The exact optimization method, thresholds, training data, stopping policy, and adaptation schedule remain unspecified.

A further assistant distinction separates **delivery learning** from **concept learning**. Improving wording, view selection, examples, or preload should not silently rewrite what is true or discard inconvenient obligations. This is a proposed integrity boundary, not a specified learning architecture.

The economics here concern Projector's prospective operation and evaluation. There are no measured savings, implementation-cost estimates, or handoff-generation budgets. Neither caching nor adaptation has yet justified its added machinery against simpler context delivery.

### CTXVIEW/C7: Evaluate interpretive stability through behavior

The user proposes simplified technical English and evaluation-driven optimization of concept prose. The need is consistent interpretation despite model-specific associations, not literary polish.

T4 suggests consistent terminology, explicit conditions and scope, and separating requirements, examples, hypotheses, and defaults. A targeted contrast could prevent an incorrect association: “ownership” might mean lifecycle/change responsibility rather than exclusive access to a file. Such non-examples are candidate improvements to test, not universal rules.

The central evaluation distinction is **behavior enabled versus clarity asserted**. Proposed adapter checks ask whether the recipient places behavior correctly, rejects a plausible prohibited implementation, requests lifecycle detail before a dependent change, and retains performance when superficial example details change.

Evaluate the composed packet, not only each concept in isolation: a locally clear paragraph may become redundant or misleading beside another view. The source does not prescribe a prose optimizer, establish a controlled-language standard for Projector, or show invariant comprehension across models.

## 4. Evaluation and evidence boundaries

T4 proposes comparing ordinary file-based context, plain retrieval, compiled views, and compiled views with disclosure frontiers and incremental supplementation on a real repository change involving interacting concepts. Task, model, tools, and verification criteria would be held constant. Outcomes include correctness, missed constraints, unnecessary exploration, total tokens, and elapsed time, with a reset and a deliberately omitted but necessary detail.

The discriminating claim is whether structured views and supplementation preserve or improve task behavior at better total cost, not whether a hand-prepared packet can succeed once. The source identifies confounds including silent failures, compaction, overlapping concepts, and excessive compression. **No trials were run.** This suggested comparison does not by itself isolate long-run adaptation, learned-backend value, or scaling; no separate discriminating evaluation for those claims was specified.

T4 supplied related-work pointers: ASD-STE100; Anthropic's “Effective context engineering for AI agents”; ACON (`arXiv:2510.00615`); GEPA (`arXiv:2507.19457`); ACE (`arXiv:2510.04618`); and ACE-GraphRAG (`arXiv:2608.01269`). It described overlap in controlled prose, retrieval, compression, prompt optimization, and evolving context. Underlying sources and verification records are absent here. T4 also asserted ACE-GraphRAG had been withdrawn and explicitly declined to use its reported performance as established evidence. These are provenance leads, not independently validated support or a novelty determination.

## 5. Integration map and unresolved tensions

| Connection | Possible integration; distinction to retain |
|---|---|
| C1 → C2/C3 | Semantic handles can request views and expansion without fixing storage. Syntax is not the rendering capability. |
| C2/C4 ↔ Execution Capsules | Context compilation might extend bounded work contracts rather than duplicate them; actual baseline needs verification. |
| C3/C5 → C6 | Omission metadata and context receipts enable selective delivery. Delivered content is not understood content. |
| C7 ↔ C6 | Outcome-based evaluation can improve delivery, but optimization must not become an alternative authority over concept truth. |
| Persistent conception / Interior Views | Shared substrate and perspective rendering may overlap. Learned conception, a holon's self-monitoring controller, and a worker's context packet are not equivalent. |

The deeper connection, stated in T4, is to project the same semantic identities into implementation artifacts and into the working perspectives of implementers and reviewers. That does not establish identical representation requirements or independent reviewer correctness. The chief unresolved tensions are narrow views versus preserved obligations, recursive specialization versus overhead, adaptive delivery versus simpler retrieval, and rich persistent concepts versus an initially textual backing.

## 6. Synthesis closure

### Core thesis from this thread

Purpose-built agent context could become an evaluated projection of shared concepts, with explicit omission boundaries and incremental expansion, instead of an incidental result of document accumulation. Its advantage remains a hypothesis.

### Load-bearing insights and distinctions to preserve

The user's expressed goals are perspective-renderable concepts, precise communication, specialized agents, selective supplementation, and efficiency through feedback. Preserve concept versus view (C2), actionable omission (C3), delivery versus comprehension (C5), and outcomes versus query counts (C6). Assistant refinements, including decision-conditioned views and canonical child rendering, remain proposals rather than user mandates.

### Candidate mechanisms, not sacred architecture

`§`, bracket grammar, Markdown files, a Semantic Context Compiler, disclosure stubs, context ledgers, caches, and adaptive policies are replaceable. Their names do not require one subsystem apiece. Alternatives should address the underlying purposes without quietly reducing the ambition to document search alone.

### Questions the final synthesis must resolve

Architecture-changing uncertainties concern the concept's backing representation, authority between source and optimized views, ownership within Projector, and whether compilation/adaptation earns its cost. Disclosure reliability and the meaning of context-instance state need evaluation. Exact syntax, cache formats, and numerical preload thresholds can wait; the source has not resolved them.

### Relationship to current Projector

The proposed delta extends semantic projection into agent working context, potentially through existing Execution Capsules and relevance machinery. Verify the reported baseline before declaring anything missing, replaceable, or already implemented. This thread establishes no repository or plugin changes.

### Suggested synthesis posture

Treat CTXVIEW as user intent, source-grounded reasoning, and testable proposals, not a binding specification. Merge, simplify, substitute, challenge, or reject mechanisms where warranted. Preserve the richer-concept seed without treating it as a prerequisite already chosen. Shared background claims and repeated assistant descriptions are not independent corroboration; synthesis should not concatenate all named machinery.
