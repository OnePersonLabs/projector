## What was done

The archive was inventoried and processed without executing its embedded prompts or code. Source addresses were retained. Exact normalized turn and rolling-word fingerprints located copied passages. Bounded source reads and editorial reconciliation organized definitions, rationale, hypotheses, alternatives, and research into canonical topic pages.

The archive's research compilation had 136 annotated entries. Normalizing the primary reference consolidated these into 120 canonical records, with aliases retaining all original entry identifiers. Repeated annotations were editorially merged. This does not prove that every distinct URL denotes a distinct publication or that every annotation is correct.

The main knowledge is organized by meaning rather than by source. Source audit and provenance are optional reading. Original chats are not copied into the output pack.

## Editorial boundaries

Conversation filler, repeated copied passages, old output-format negotiations, sponsors, motivational digressions, and redundant highlights are removed from the working knowledge. Full paper reference lists, test-spec appendices, illustrative code listings, and every original table are not reproduced verbatim. The deeper eval bibliography retains its useful annotations through canonical records.

Current conception, reusable reasons, and live alternatives are distinguished. Categorical practitioner rules such as always restarting a run or always using the strongest reviewer are retained as conditional approaches, not promoted to universal requirements. Proposals described as existing Projector features remain unverified implementation claims because the repository was not inspected.

The new session-relative routing policy is explicitly separated from the archived design synthesis. It does not silently turn a past Astra-owner recommendation into an accepted permanent hierarchy.

## Evidence and verification limits

Research annotations are supplied-source reports, not a fresh systematic verification campaign. The original compilation's statement that its references were verified is not adopted as this response's own work. Numerical findings, product status, licenses, and unsupported marketing claims remain qualified.

Current native Codex inheritance/configuration and shared-allowance behavior were checked against official documentation for the routing page. The user's installed binary, account entitlements, exact exposed tools, and effective runtime model settings were not inspected.

Mechanical validation can establish source accounting, package integrity, local navigation, and selected duplication properties. Editorial spot checks can inspect important retained distinctions. Neither proves complete semantic losslessness. Keep the original ZIP as the archival record.
