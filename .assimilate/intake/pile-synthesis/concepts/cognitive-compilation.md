# Cognitive compilation

`§cognitive-compilation` · Convert useful reasoning into cheaper reusable capability.

## Core

A successful investigation can leave behind more than an answer. It can produce a selector, executable obligation, validator, counterexample, transformation, task-specific skill, context view, or qualified routing rule that makes the next similar task require less fresh reasoning.

This is the bridge between repeated expensive judgment and increasingly reliable mechanical work. The goal is not to preserve every reasoning transcript. It is to preserve the reusable structure that actually helped.

## The compilation target

A discovered dependency may become a current-state query. A recurring semantic mistake may become a perturbation fixture. A stable rewrite may become a codemod. A repeated interpretation problem may become a contrastive example in a context packet. A transferable procedure may become a skill with evaluated triggers and scripts.

Choose the lowest-burden artifact that preserves the demonstrated advantage. Do not train a model for an exact operation a deterministic tool already performs reliably. Do not encode an uncertain architectural judgment as a permanent rule merely because it occurred once.

## Candidate pipeline

```text
Observed task and outcome
→ isolate the reusable distinction or operation
→ propose a compact artifact
→ test on independent and changed cases
→ retain only if it improves complete outcomes
→ monitor applicability and retire when obsolete
```

A failed run can be more informative than a successful one because it exposes a missing condition. A success-only recipe may accidentally preserve luck or benchmark-specific assumptions.

## Learnability as a useful lens

Information volume and usefulness are not the same. A carefully selected contrast can make a distinction easier to learn than a large undifferentiated history. This motivates curriculum design and compact operational representations.

“Epiplexity” appears as an inspiration for thinking about learnable structure under resource limits. The conceptual benefit does not require importing a formal metric or treating a difficult-to-measure quantity as a practical scheduler score.

## Portability requires testing

A skill can be readable by another model without improving its performance. It can add unnecessary steps, conflict with existing behavior, or rely on tools the recipient lacks.

Qualify the artifact on the receiving model and actual task environment. Include near-miss triggers, missing prerequisites, changed examples, and failure cases. Improvements on the authoring model do not establish transfer.

## Independence and authority

The system that proposes an artifact should not be the sole judge of whether it helps. Keep accepted obligations distinct from generated procedures. A promising rule can remain a proposal until it passes a bounded comparison.

Execution traces are evidence of what occurred, not automatically correct explanations of why. Avoid preserving unnecessary private reasoning or raw sensitive inputs when a compact test or operational rule suffices.

## Stop compiling when it does not pay

A rarely recurring problem may not justify a permanent artifact. An adaptation that lowers initial tokens but increases repair is not an improvement. Apply [work economics](work-economics.md) to acquisition, validation, maintenance, and retirement.
