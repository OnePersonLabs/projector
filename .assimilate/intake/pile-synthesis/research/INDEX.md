The concept pages contain the Projector design. These research pages explain the donor evidence and its limits. A paper demonstrating a failure mode does not establish that Projector fixes it. A vendor case study does not establish universal savings. A transcript making a large performance claim is not a controlled benchmark.

The annotated catalogue retains deeper bibliographic material in one canonical record per normalized primary reference, with aliases linking repeated mentions. It preserves the dated source annotations and their caveats rather than treating them as new measurements.
