## “Becoming the concept” → activating a working perspective

Take **undo**.

An outside description might say:

> “Undo reverses a previous action.”

An inside representation would make a whole set of distinctions available together: what counts as an action; whose action it was; which consequences belong to it; which consequences are reversible; what later actions depend on it; and what must remain untouched.

Under that interpretation, to “become undo” is to temporarily organize attention and prediction around those distinctions.

The system isn’t just retrieving sentences containing *undo*. It is examining the situation through undo’s structure:

> “That change looks small in the code, but it changes which consequences I can reverse without destroying something unrelated.”

**The perspective is defined by what it can distinguish, predict, and preserve—not by whether it speaks in the first person.**

There is a relevant cognitive-science precedent here. Barsalou’s *Perceptual Symbol Systems* proposes conceptual representations that can generate simulations, rather than treating knowledge only as descriptions in an abstract symbol system. My engineering translation would be:

## This connects directly to your history-and-training idea

Your earlier thought about squashing developer flip-flopping into a usable present model fits here—but I would preserve a distinction:

**Compress the current understanding; retain access to the reasons it is current.**

For our design, I’d separate three things:

**What we intend** is the accepted behavior and its scope. **What we believe** is the current explanatory or predictive model. **What we have observed** is evidence from implementations, tests, and actual behavior.

Those must influence one another without collapsing into one another. Otherwise the system can “preserve coherence” by changing its interpretation until whatever it just implemented counts as correct.

## Where this would become empty theater

I see three decisive traps.

**First-person narration without changed capability.** “I am Undo, guardian of reversibility” buys us nothing by itself. We need better predictions, better questions, better impact detection, or cheaper reasoning.

**Self-preservation mistaken for conceptual integrity.** A concept must be allowed to split, merge, become obsolete, or acquire a revised purpose. Protecting yesterday’s implementation would be the opposite of useful evolution.

**“Direct cognition” treated as free computation.** The achievable target is avoiding repeated reconstruction by retaining useful organization. We still have to establish that an explicit or learned representation actually helps the reasoning system use that organization.

Otherwise, yes: we have built a committee of talking README files.
