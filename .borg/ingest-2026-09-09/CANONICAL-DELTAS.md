## One new requirement, because its ownership is distinct

Proposed key/ID: `state-bound-contributions` / `requirement:state-bound-contributions`.

Title: “Join reusable contributions into one concrete change.”

Statement: “Allow a host to prepare bounded contributions against saved Projector context. Each contribution identifies its work contract, input dependencies, predecessor results, producer attempt, output and evidence content, effect scope, semantic owners, unresolved conditions and required or optional status. Before capture, contributions are host-owned candidate evidence without mutation or completion authority. Import frozen bytes, validate currentness and compatibility, and join them into one exact proposal through the existing repository change lifecycle. Bind complete versioned contribution-envelope hashes, including provenance and omissions, and join contract/result digests into proposal, capture and approved-plan identities; reauthenticate required attachments for plan, approval, new apply and current acceptance. Equal edits and repository state with different admitted evidence require different capture/plan identities and cannot reuse approval. Missing required results, changed dependencies, unresolved shared-contract conflicts or substituted artifacts prevent readiness. Recover previous mutation from its authenticated transaction proof; missing contributions alone cannot prevent safe rollback or authenticated historical publication and cannot justify rerunning committed effects. Derive continuation from current context and lifecycle evidence without introducing another workflow store.”

Proposed scope: control-plane contribution admission/capture/continuation modules, their pure core contracts and the CLI/plugin input/output paths that actually consume them. Use exact observed path scopes during implementation; retain explicit unrealized commitment until then.

Relations: governed by existing durable-meaning, pre-edit-relevance, scoped-invalidation and evidence-bound-completion requirements; demonstrated by the scenarios below. Use the repository's existing supported relation kinds, rather than inventing a vocabulary solely for this document.

No new requirement is needed for 'graph engineering', 'anti-slop', 'domain agents', 'geometric reasoning', or 'long-running harnesses'. Those names describe donor packaging, not new Projector meaning owners.

## Transaction and migration sequence

1. Reconcile retained context; retrieve selected existing identities and governing meaning at the current implementation state.
2. Accept precise requirement/scenario refinements and explicit future obligations; preserve every current origin and stable identity.
3. For the next slice, accept its concern/decision/authority and concrete non-advisory products in the same supported lifecycle transaction. Do not manufacture successful evidence for unimplemented behavior.
4. Implement the slice, collect its actual validation and reconcile. Expand path bindings only to actual consumers; mapping is not fulfillment.
5. Review later evidence against scoped reconsideration triggers. Future commitments remain even when a bounded release lacks their implementation.
