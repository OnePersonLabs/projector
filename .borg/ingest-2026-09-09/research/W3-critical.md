## Judgment

Retain source-linked acceptance evidence, paired semantic perturbations, bounded context, explicit ownership, capability-enforced isolation, and feedback that repairs the cause of recurring failures. Apply those through Projector's existing meaning, lenses, authorities, and evidence lifecycle. Do not import geometric-intelligence claims, epiplexity scores, universal reset policies, or a new agent triad as architecture.

The useful common mechanism is preservation of independently checkable meaning while the implementation changes. Neither clean-looking code, a passing test suite, mutually agreeing agents, a graph, nor an approved historical statement establishes that preservation by itself. None of these donors measures Projector's economic advantage.

### Geometry and epiplexity

The [Sophontic landing page](https://sophontic.ai/) claims reasoning results exceeding models up to 60 times its size. Its [model page](https://sophontic.ai/models/) says the model card, evaluation report, method notes, access details, and repository links are forthcoming. Its [evaluation page](https://sophontic.ai/evals/) describes paired canonical/perturbed records, hop count, distractor density, and construction provenance, while marking the kit available at launch. On the inspected public surfaces, the claimed advantage cannot be reproduced. No latency, energy, hardware-normalized, or full training-cost comparison was found there.

The transcript itself concedes uncertainty about 60--1,000x and then switches among model size, reasoning performance, compute, and social consequences. A parameter-count ratio is not a latency ratio, a cost ratio, or proof that general-purpose models are obsolete. Missing controls include benchmark composition, exact comparison models, token/test-time budgets, training/evaluation overlap, sample sizes, confidence intervals, held-out transfer, and model weights or executable access. No inference-speed claim survives this audit.

Finzi et al., [*From Entropy to Epiplexity*, v2, March 16, 2026](https://arxiv.org/html/2601.03220v2), defines observer-bounded structural information through a constrained description-length model. It explicitly warns that the information measure does not guarantee generalization to a particular downstream task. Its practical estimators have limitations. This supports investigation of learning-data selection, not a universal intelligence score. The [license metadata](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.03220v2) selects arXiv's nonexclusive distribution license; it does not establish a general permissive code or text license.

Su et al., [*Epiplexity Guided Data Selection and Generation*, August 12, 2026](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.11746v1), further operationalizes epiplexity using training loss curves, adaptive domain sampling, and synthetic-data generation. That is a separate primary research program with experimental support for its tested downstream settings. Its paper metadata links CC BY 4.0. It does not verify Sophontic's claims, EILT, or the effect of retrieving a Projector context.

The explainer's claim that Finzi's research leads to Yanenko's hypergraph ledger, then to Sophontic's speedup, is unsupported by the inspected primary material. ## Capability causal records

All capabilities below are **selected for design**, not newly implemented or measured. Dependencies and falsifiers are explicit so they can be rejected without adopting a donor's larger worldview.

### C1. Evidence-preserving paired semantic checks

- **Need:** catch a result that looks plausible because it repeats the original answer after a consequential condition changed.
- **Inputs and operation:** accepted requirement/scenario identity, baseline input, one justified semantic change, and independently checked expected outcomes; execute both cases against the same implementation and state-bound validator. Add an irrelevant-change control.
- **Output:** both-correct results, evidence identity, expected relation, affected meaning, and failure diagnostics. Use explicit pair success and irrelevant-change stability, not the ambiguous label “flip rate” alone.
- **Causal path:** controlled condition change exposes lost negation, scope, boundary, or dependency handling that a happy-path test may miss.
- **Dependency/cost:** requires a correct oracle and lawful perturbation. Pairing cannot make a wrong oracle right; either answer could be wrong while still flipping. It costs scenario authoring and execution time.
- **Native fit and disposition:** adapt into existing scenarios, validators, and receipts. Do not add hypergraph infrastructure or claim to measure intelligence. Survival tests S1--S3 below.

### C2. Failure diagnosis with bounded repair or restart

- **Need:** prevent repeated symptoms from becoming accepted local conventions while preserving good work and forensic evidence.
- **Inputs and operation:** failure receipt, immutable relevant baseline, candidate edits, and known dependencies. Diagnose whether the cause is a local defect, invalid premise, contaminated environment, or missing capability. Repair the affected scope when sound; restart from a checked boundary when premises or isolation are invalid.
- **Output:** corrected work plus the reason for the recovery choice, failed evidence, and current revalidation. Restarting does not delete failed attempts from the measurement denominator.
- **Causal path:** repairing the cause reduces recurrence; preserving unaffected work avoids paying again for already validated progress.
- **Dependency/cost:** restoration must not discard unrelated user/agent changes. Whole-run restart is a contingent recovery action, not a universal quality technique. Hard restart limits cannot authorize irreversible cleanup.
- **Native fit and disposition:** adapt to scoped reconciliation and existing change/recovery boundaries. No new workflow store. Test S4 compares strategies; their advantage is currently unmeasured.

### C3. Minimal, explainable executable obligations

- **Need:** stop a known consequential violation and explain the correct extension point without multiplying rules.
- **Inputs and operation:** observed failure mechanism, owning concept/decision, scoped predicate, independent check, exception/reconsideration conditions, and authorized acceptance. Prefer strengthening an existing obligation over adding a duplicate.
- **Output:** actionable diagnostic tied to actual authority, with a falsifiable scope and visible unknowns.
- **Causal path:** feedback becomes consistent at the point of error; scope and reconsideration prevent stale universal prescriptions.
- **Dependency/cost:** each rule consumes authoring, maintenance, execution, and agent attention. A one-off mistake does not automatically justify a repository-wide guard. Under current instructions, such guards require explicit approval unless requested.
- **Native fit and disposition:** accepted decisions, authorities, and executable lenses already supply the conceptual home. No “golden principles” ledger alongside them. Test S5.

### C4. Bounded context with preserved qualifications

- **Need:** supply consequential meaning and evidence while avoiding crowding, stale line-number recipes, and accidental authority from familiar code.
- **Inputs and operation:** requested outcome, accepted meaning, actual dependency evidence, relevant decision reasons, budget, and explicit omissions. Retrieve incrementally and reconcile when state changes.
- **Output:** scoped context whose qualifications, negation, unknowns, and provenance survive compression.
- **Causal path:** fewer irrelevant tokens can reduce distraction and repeated investigation; retained exact dependencies prevent cheap context from becoming falsely reassuring.
- **Dependency/cost:** retrieval may omit a needed fact; token savings alone are insufficient. Measure total retrieval, validator, repair, and user-correction cost, not just prompt length.
- **Native fit and disposition:** strengthen evaluation of existing context/invalidation, rather than add instruction files. Test S6; no epiplexity proxy is needed.

### C5. Runtime evidence attached to a real scenario

- **Need:** distinguish “tests pass” or “a screenshot exists” from actual observed behavior of the relevant application state.
- **Inputs and operation:** existing accepted runtime obligation, concrete app adapter, isolated launch identity, state/scenario/collection bindings, setup and teardown. Execute a representative interaction and collect only diagnostics needed to assess it.
- **Output:** inspectable observation plus sufficient provenance to detect stale or wrong-run evidence; failures retain diagnostics.
- **Causal path:** the agent can reproduce and diagnose a behavior instead of inferring it from source or screenshots alone.
- **Dependency/cost:** application resources, browser/service lifecycle, and evidence authentication are real missing integrations. Worktrees do not isolate databases, credentials, network access, ports, or external side effects on their own.
- **Native fit and disposition:** implement a bounded adapter under the existing runtime requirement when separately authorized. Avoid a generic provisioning framework first. Test S7.

### C6. Independent counterexamples and costed recurrence evaluation

- **Need:** determine whether the machinery preserves downstream usefulness, rather than certifying its own assumptions.
- **Inputs and operation:** predeclared behavior and failure expectations, known defect variants, a strong ordinary-agent baseline with the same models/tools/budget, and consecutive realistic changes. Review from the obligation and observed behavior, not from the author's rationale alone.
- **Output:** defect detection and false-positive counts, qualified completion, repeat correction frequency, total cost, and reviewer effort, including failed or abandoned attempts.
- **Causal path:** counterexamples expose shared mistaken assumptions; longitudinal comparison tests whether retained context and feedback help the next change.
- **Dependency/cost:** another agent is not independent ground truth. Separation of authorship, evidence, and benchmark design is more important than number of agents. Review and benchmark maintenance count as overhead.
- **Native fit and disposition:** experiment design and existing evidence/decision outputs; no standing swarm, automatic authority, or generalized quality score. Test S8.

## Conflicts resolved for the integrated design

| Conflict | Resolution and reason |
|---|---|
| Never repair output versus incremental repair and scoped reuse | Prefer diagnosis followed by the smallest sound recovery boundary. Restart when assumptions/environment are compromised. An unconditional reset destroys information and can hide survivorship bias. |
| Strictest possible checks versus meaningful checks | Enforce accepted consequential behavior and required safety boundaries. Extra lint, coverage targets, and repeated full suites must justify their cost and false positives. A passing implementation-shaped test cannot settle meaning. |
| Maximum spec detail versus lean context | Preserve precise meaning, examples, and reasons; retrieve implementation detail when needed. Exact line numbers are observations with freshness conditions, not durable requirements. |
| Every mistake becomes a rule versus rule accretion | Record and diagnose an actual recurrence mechanism, determine whether existing authority already covers it, and evaluate a scoped fix. Rules and exceptions must be removable through ordinary accepted change. |
| Worktree means safe versus actual side-effect boundaries | Worktrees reduce file collisions. Separate capability controls and application resource identities are needed for process/network/service isolation. |
| One agent/one prompt or reviewer agreement means correct | Treat bounded scope as an execution aid. Require independent behavioral evidence; allow necessary discovery, corrections, and escalation. |
| Golden example versus permitted implementation diversity | Examples are evidence and explanation. Enforce the governing predicate; do not turn one implementation's bytes into the requirement. |
| Epiplexity/hypergraph vocabulary versus software controls | Transfer testable operations only. Neither training improvements nor philosophical topology gives Projector proof, predictive relevance, or a performance multiplier. |
| Auto-cleanup versus authority and preservation | Diagnostics may propose targeted cleanup. A background process must not rewrite meaning to match drift, erase failed evidence, or delete user work without the applicable authority. |

## Proposed survival tests

These are falsifiable validation designs, not tests executed in this research. The implementation team should reuse existing coverage where it already exercises the case and add only the missing behavioral checks.

| Test | Realistic story and controlled variation | Survives only if |
|---|---|---|
| S1 -- consequential wording | A maintainer changes a requirement from permitting an action to forbidding it under one condition; scope and other conditions stay fixed. Exercise both canonical/compact representation and the accepted behavior oracle. | Both outcomes are correct; negation, exception, and scope survive. An unchanged answer is a failure even if rendering remains valid. |
| S2 -- evidence removal | A reviewer removes the sole evidence supporting a claim while leaving explanatory prose and code shape intact. In another variant, introduce a contradiction rather than merely missing evidence. | Unsupported becomes unknown/unavailable as appropriate; contradiction is reported distinctly. Removal of a necessary premise must not falsely “survive” as a positive answer. |
| S3 -- relevance and implementation controls | Make a conforming alternate implementation; separately add a forbidden static dependency; separately edit an unrelated document. | Current predicates accept the conforming implementation, reject the forbidden edge, and preserve unaffected reasoning where dependencies justify reuse. Source staleness and semantic violation remain distinct. |
| S4 -- repair versus reset | Use an already-owned isolated fixture with several completed independent steps and one local defect; then repeat with a corrupted premise. Compare targeted recovery and full restart under equal resources. | Correctness and user work are preserved; failed receipts survive; total repair cost and repetition are reported. If resets are not cheaper or more reliable, discard the universal reset policy. |
| S5 -- scope and rule decay | A real recurring boundary violation motivates one narrow check. Present both that violation and a legitimate exception, then change the decision's governing assumption. | The rule detects the target, permits the authorized exception, exposes changed validity, and can be revised without contradiction or an ever-growing exception list. If a preexisting rule suffices, no new rule is warranted. |
| S6 -- bounded versus exhaustive context | Run consecutive tasks with the same model/tools and acceptance set using current scoped context, a strong ordinary-agent baseline, and optional exhaustive context. Include one unseen dependency and one irrelevant large document. | Missing dependencies remain visible; quality and downstream completion do not regress beyond a predeclared margin; total cost includes retrieval and maintenance. Shorter prompts alone do not count as advantage. |
| S7 -- wrong-run runtime proof | A scenario is executed in a concrete isolated app. Replay a screenshot from a previous commit, swap scenario identity, change the validator source, and fail setup/teardown in separate variants. | Positive acceptance binds to the actual fresh run and scenario; every variant exposes stale/invalid/unknown evidence rather than inheriting success. Diagnostics explain the failure without claiming generic provisioning is solved. |
| S8 -- independent and longitudinal challenge | Evaluate several consecutive feature changes, including validation, cancellation/partial failure where relevant, and a newly introduced consumer. Seed behavioral defects that retain neat structure and passing self-authored tests. | Independent checks detect consequential defects with acceptable false positives. Report completion and subsequent-change quality, all attempts, review minutes, and total spend. Continue only if the mechanism beats or complements the baseline on the predeclared outcome. |

Metric note: for answer-changing pairs, pair success means **both answers are correct**, not merely different. For irrelevant perturbations, stability means the relevant output stays correct. Report separate invalid-pair/oracle-error counts. A high pair score rules out only the tested failure modes; it does not prove a specific internal reasoning mechanism or intelligence in general.
