# Roadmap: Projector

## Overview

Projector is delivered as one dependency-ordered semantic control plane: a deterministic, fine-grained foundation first proves the mandatory misplaced-script causal loop; subsequent slices broaden semantic reasoning, governance, architecture, topology, completion, execution, host integration, modernization, hardening, and external surfaces. The final phase dogfoods the complete system and makes the GSD bootstrap and `PROJECTOR_SPEC/` safely removable only after canonical import and semantic-equivalence proof.

## Phases

**Phase Numbering:** Sequential integer phases are planned MVP work. This locked roadmap contains no deferred or optional phases.

- [ ] **Phase 1: Slice 0 — Foundation and Correctness Substrate** - Establish the deterministic, secure, fine-grained semantic substrate required by every later slice.
- [ ] **Phase 2: Slice 1 — Mandatory Misplaced-Script Loop** - Prove the first end-to-end governed repair on the mandatory misplaced-script fixture.
- [ ] **Phase 3: Slice 2 — Semantic Signatures, Invalidation, and Backdating** - Make reuse, invalidation, and historical evidence exact, scoped, and assurance-aware.
- [ ] **Phase 4: Slice 3 — Behavioral Intent, Identity Resolution, and Relevance Engine** - Compile change meaning into canonical behavioral identities and bounded relevance before planning work.
- [ ] **Phase 5: Slice 4 — Governance Robustness and Semantic Representations** - Make governance deterministic and compile canonical semantics into faithful derived representations.
- [ ] **Phase 6: Slice 5 — Progressive Architecture Commitment** - Turn material architectural concerns into scoped, evidence-bound decisions and consequences.
- [ ] **Phase 7: Slice 6 — Broader Analyzers and Relevance/Divergence Topology** - Broaden deterministic analyzers and topology while keeping confidence, capability, and proof boundaries honest.
- [ ] **Phase 8: Slice 7 — Coverage Completion and Cleanup** - Make coverage, reconciliation, completion, cleanup, and divergence claims explicit and resumable.
- [ ] **Phase 9: Slice 8 — Full Semantic Change Compiler and Packet Executor** - Compile semantic change into immutable plans and execute bounded packets with recovery and certification.
- [ ] **Phase 10: Slice 9 — Codex, Claude Code, and MCP Integrations** - Provide capability-aware Codex, Claude Code, and MCP access to bounded, state-governed work.
- [ ] **Phase 11: Slice 10 — Modernization and Migration Overlays** - Route modernization through normal governance with safe migration overlays and upstream-first repair.
- [ ] **Phase 12: Slice 11 — Watch, CI, Security, and Hardening** - Harden continuous operation, recovery, reporting, CI, and security boundaries for daily use.
- [ ] **Phase 13: Slice 12 — External Surface Adapters** - Connect external surfaces through revisioned, capability-aware adapter contracts without weakening the core.
- [ ] **Phase 14: Dogfooding, Public Release, and GSD-to-Projector Handoff** - Prove the complete system on itself, release it honestly, and complete the verified GSD-to-Projector migration.
