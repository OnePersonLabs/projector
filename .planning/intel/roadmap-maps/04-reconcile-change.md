## Phase success-criteria contributions

- **Phase 04** — A request yields bounded intent, explicit implementation-proposal status, resolved semantic identity, stable Requirement/Scenario deltas, and a relevance seed without authorizing mutation.
- **Phase 05** — Exceptions and inferred deltas preserve authority boundaries, while specifications and Gherkin remain governed Representation Projections rather than canonical storage.
- **Phase 06** — Material changes activate proportionate facets and pass an architecture preflight with no unresolved blocking-now frontier concern unless a policy-authorized override is recorded.
- **Phase 07** — Relevance scouting and Impact Closure use encoded topology and bounded query dependencies, distinguish relevance from impact, expose frontier provenance, and never infer exhaustive absence from unobservable lanes.
- **Phase 08** — Reconciliation converges deterministically, emits explainable divergence and multidimensional coverage, preserves independent proofs under partial failures, and claims completion only within an explicit observable boundary.
- **Phase 09** — The compiler produces immutable, state-bound plans and coordinated work packets; the single-writer executor checkpoints, validates, reconciles actual impact, handles surprises without authority expansion, and emits distinct receipts and certificates.
- **Phase 11** — Migration overlays progress through explicit lifecycle states with selector-visible temporary obligations, validation and rollback criteria, and exit-criteria-based residue detection.
- **Phase 12** — Interrupted transactions recover deterministically with cross-system durability and failure certificates anchored to the last durable checkpoint.
- **Phase 14** — Release and GSD handoff can export and link content-addressed change certificates without committing them by default.
