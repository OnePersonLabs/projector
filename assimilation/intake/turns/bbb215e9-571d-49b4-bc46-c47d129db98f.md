### The question I'd put to the repo

> Starting from first principles, determine what irreducible capabilities Projector must possess to make indefinitely continued agentic development converge toward coherent software rather than accumulated entropy. Then independently determine what capabilities the current architecture claims to provide and what capabilities the implementation demonstrably provides. Identify missing primitives, redundant machinery, false proxies, mechanisms solving symptoms rather than causes, unjustified complexity, internal contradictions, and places where Projector itself exhibits the failure modes it exists to prevent. Do not propose implementation changes until the causal model is established.

That last sentence is extremely important.

The current Projector design has accumulated a *lot* of nouns:

It has to be determined by asking of every component:

\[
\boxed{\text{What irreducible information does this preserve that would otherwise be lost?}}

Because I increasingly suspect the breakthrough isn't going to be another clever subsystem.

Your concern, therefore, isn't evidence that Projector failed. It's actually the perfect test of its thesis:

> **Can the thing designed to detect semantic drift discover that its own development has semantically drifted?**
